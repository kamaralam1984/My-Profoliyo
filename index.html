SEQUENCE BREAKDOWN

The Method — Five-Act Process

Every engagement runs through the same five acts, in the same order, every time.

I

Understand

Clearly define the requirement or business problem.

II

Analyze

Identify gaps in the current system, workflow, documents or output.

III

Design

Structure a practical solution, features, priorities and action points.

IV

Execute

Support team/developer coordination and implementation follow-up.

V

Report

Convert results, risks, pending work and next steps into clear documentation.
